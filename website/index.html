Key Transit Features: 100% Offline Route Planner & BMRCL Station Guide

Commuting through Bengaluru's dense traffic and underground metro tunnels presents unique connectivity challenges. NammaRide is engineered with native offline caching, requiring 0 KB cellular data once downloaded.

1. Satellite Geo GPS Nearest Station Tracking

Using on-device satellite GNSS/GPS, NammaRide pinpoints your exact distance to the nearest metro station in real time. It calculates walking duration and direct street navigation without querying third-party cloud servers, ensuring 100% user privacy and zero battery drain.

2. Turnstile Gate QR Ticket Max Brightness Mode

Save BMRCL WhatsApp or digital QR tickets directly inside the app. When you approach automated fare collection (AFC) turnstile gates, open the ticket with one tap. NammaRide automatically cranks screen brightness to maximum and keeps the screen awake for rapid barcode scanning.

3. Direct WhatsApp Official BMRCL Chatbot Booking

Plan your station-to-station trip and tap "Book Ticket on WhatsApp". NammaRide formats the exact originating station, destination station, and passenger count into a pre-filled message sent to BMRCL's official verified chatbot (+91 81055 56677).

First & Last Train Operating Schedules (Bengaluru Metro)

Namma Metro trains operate with clockwork precision throughout the week:

  • Monday to Saturday: First train starts at 05:00 AM from all terminal stations. Last train departs at 11:05 PM.
  • Sundays & General Holidays: Services commence at 07:00 AM. Last train departs at 11:05 PM.
  • Peak-Hour Frequency: Trains run every 3.5 to 5 minutes during morning peak (08:30 AM – 11:00 AM) and evening peak (05:00 PM – 08:30 PM).
  • Non-Peak Frequency: Trains run every 7 to 10 minutes during afternoon and late-night periods.

Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Majestic Interchange Walk Guide

Majestic is Bengaluru's central transit nexus, seamlessly connecting the underground Purple Line and elevated Green Line. Switching between lines takes approximately 4 to 5 minutes:

  • Changing Purple ➔ Green Line: Alight at Platform 1 (towards Challaghatta) or Platform 2 (towards Whitefield). Follow the green floor tiles and overhead arrows upstairs to the Green Line platforms (Platform 1 for Madavara, Platform 2 for Silk Institute).
  • Changing Green ➔ Purple Line: Alight from Green Line and take the central escalators downstairs to the underground Purple Line concourse.
  • Accessibility: High-capacity elevators link both levels for elderly passengers and commuters with luggage.

Divyangjan Accessibility Directory & Commuter Facilities

Every operational station on the BMRCL network complies with Indian Harmonised Guidelines for Universal Accessibility:

  • Tactile Pathways: Continuous yellow embossed tactile tiles guide visually impaired commuters from street entry gates directly to train door boarding points.
  • Elevators & Ramps: Dedicated wheelchair-accessible lifts equipped with Braille control buttons and audible floor announcements.
  • Safety & Medical: First-aid kits, automated external defibrillators (AED), and emergency passenger alarm buttons installed on all platform screen gates and train carriages.
